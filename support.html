Support for the Roost beta.

Get in touch

Email roost.support@dakotaraymond.com, or send feedback through TestFlight. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Roost build number and what happened. Please keep passwords, email codes and private route files out of your report.

Recording a ride

Allow Precise Location in iPhone Settings. Start a ride in Roost, then pause or finish when you need to. Recording works without cell service; map imagery and account features need a connection. GPS estimates can vary under trees and in canyons.

If iOS stops the app or you force quit it, reopen Roost to recover the last saved checkpoint as a paused ride. Resume explicitly. Do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot: saved routes and photos are stored on this phone.

Signing in

Use your email and password. Enter the latest verification code from your inbox when confirming an account or resetting your password. Check spam if mail is missing. If a request is rate limited, wait and use an already-delivered code instead of repeatedly requesting another.

Friends and rankings

Add friends by their exact username. They must accept your request before you can share rankings. Turn on leaderboard participation to share ride totals. Choose “Rode with” when saving or editing a ride to build shared friendship history.

Deleting data

You can delete a ride from its detail screen after confirming. Delete your account through the account menu to remove cloud account data. Your local logbook remains until you delete its rides or remove the app.
