Your ride. Your data.

Privacy policy · Updated October 8, 2026

Roost is an iPhone app developed by Dakota Raymond for recording ATV, side-by-side and dirt bike rides. This policy describes the current beta.

Recording and photos on your phone

Roost records GPS locations, timestamps, speed and elevation during rides you start. Your routes, notes, logbook and copies of attached photos are stored on your iPhone. Roost does not upload these detailed routes, notes or photos to its account service.

Photos access is optional. You can choose individual images or allow Roost to find pictures captured between starting and finishing a ride. Removing a photo in Roost does not remove the original from your photo library. Standard device backups may include Roost’s local files.

Accounts and friends

If you create an account, our Supabase account service stores your email address, account ID, username, display name and preferences. For email accounts, your password is handled by the authentication service; Roost does not save it locally. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple supplies an account identifier, your name when available, and the email address you choose to share. Hide My Email supplies an Apple relay address. Supabase validates the Apple sign-in and stores the resulting identity; Roost never receives your Apple password.

If you opt into leaderboards, Roost uploads ride dates, vehicle category and totals such as distance, moving time and recorded peak speed. Accepted friends can compare these totals. Turning participation off hides your rankings; it does not delete your stored summaries.

Choosing friends you rode with shares the outing date, vehicle and totals with the selected accepted friends. Crew members who are already friends can also see their shared outing with each other. Crew tagging is separate from leaderboard participation. It does not share your detailed route, photos, ride title or notes.

Friend request notifications

If you allow friend request alerts, Roost stores a random installation identifier, your account ID and an Apple push device token in its Supabase service. Apple Push Notification service delivers the sender’s display name, request identifier and pending-request count to your device. Notifications do not include your ride route, email address or photos. You can disable alerts in Roost’s account menu or iPhone notification settings. Signing out stops notifications on that installation, and deleting your account removes its device registrations and delivery records. Inactive registrations expire after 90 days; delivery records are removed after seven days.

Services we use

Supabase provides authentication and the friends database. Apple provides optional Sign in with Apple, its private email relay, and push notification delivery. Resend delivers account verification and password recovery emails. Apple MapKit provides map imagery and may receive requests for the area displayed. GitHub Pages hosts our public support and privacy pages and may process website request information. These services process requests under their own privacy policies: Supabase, Resend and Apple and GitHub.

Service providers may process operational information such as network addresses and request logs to deliver and protect their services. Roost has no advertising, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics integration, and does not sell your data.

Sharing you choose

The system share sheet lets you send ride summaries, photos or GPX files to destinations you select. A GPX file can contain exact coordinates, timestamps, the ride name and altitude. Roost does not send an export automatically.

Your controls

You can change location and Photos permissions in iPhone Settings, turn automatic ride photos off in Roost, remove friends, disable leaderboard participation, edit your riding crew, and delete saved rides after confirming.

Delete your account from Roost’s account menu to remove your account, profile, friendships and uploaded summaries. Apple accounts require confirmation with the same Apple account; Roost revokes its Apple authorization before completing deletion. Signing out or deleting your account does not delete your local logbook. Delete local rides separately. Service backups and operational records may be retained according to the providers’ retention policies. Reinstalling or changing phones does not currently restore your routes from your Roost account.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion help, email roost.support@dakotaraymond.com. We will update this policy as Roost’s features and data practices change.
